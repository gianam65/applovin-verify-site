About us

Snowline Supply is an online retailer of snowboarding and winter sports equipment. We stock a small, curated range rather than a full catalogue, and every product is one our team has ridden. Orders ship from our warehouse within two business days.

Shipping & returns

Standard shipping is free on orders over $120 and takes 3–5 business days. Unused gear in original packaging can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Boards that have been ridden are covered by the manufacturer's warranty only.
